# 🌙 Lucidia

**A Speculative Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) App for Sensory Dream Interventions**

*FigBuild 2026 Hackathon Submission: Sensory Experience Tool*

Lucidia is a revolutionary, Apple-inspired mobile application that acts as the control center for next-generation Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) like *NeuroLink* and *Cortical Sync*. It goes beyond standard sleep tracking by bridging the gap between the conscious and unconscious mind. Lucidia tracks, visualizes, and directly manipulates the user's sensory experiences while they sleep—specifically targeting dreams to treat phobias, resolve trauma, and optimize cognitive restoration through an advanced 8-sense neural mapping system.

---

## 🎯 The Challenge & Our Vision

This project answers the challenge to "design a tool that tracks, measures, visualizes or quantifies an aspect of human sensory experience, and provides the ability to detect, enhance, or manipulate those same sensory inputs."

Lucidia unlocks the **subconscious sensory landscape**. By recording and decoding the multisensory experiences of dreams across all 8 human senses (visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, gustatory, proprioception, interoception, vestibular, and chronoception), Lucidia allows users to perceive the unremembered data of their own sleeping minds, and safely manipulate it to improve their waking life.

## 👁️ What can they perceive now that they couldn't before?

**The Subconscious Sensory Landscape (The Hidden Dimensions of the Dreaming Mind):**

Humans experience rich visual, tactile, and emotional stimuli while dreaming, but 90% of it is forgotten upon waking. Lucidia translates the brain's neural firing patterns during REM sleep into quantifiable data across 8 distinct sensory channels:

- **Visual** - Dream imagery intensity and color vibrancy
- **Auditory** - Sound frequencies and voice recognition
- **Tactile** - Touch, pressure, and texture sensations
- **Olfactory** - Scent detection and emotional triggers
- **Vestibular** - Spatial orientation, flying, falling sensations
- **Proprioception** - Body awareness and movement
- **Interoception** - Internal bodily sensations (heartbeat, breathing, temperature)
- **Chronoception** - Perception of time dilation and compression

Users can now "see" and "feel" what their brain was experiencing overnight. More importantly, they gain **Lucidity Awareness**—the ability to perceive and manipulate their dream state in real-time through BCI-triggered sensory overrides (e.g., injecting a calming olfactory sensation of lavender when the brain detects a stress-inducing visual in a nightmare).

## 📱 How does the tool work in everyday life? (3 Use Cases)

1. **The Phobia Treatment Protocol (Exposure Therapy):**
   *Story:* Sarah has a severe fear of spiders (arachnophobia). During a routine dream analysis, Lucidia detects recurring arachnid-like visual patterns causing heart-rate spikes. The app suggests a "Sensory Override." Sarah manually approves the intervention. The next night, when the BCI detects the nightmare forming, it overrides the tactile and visual processing centers, turning the terrifying sensation into a soft, glowing, harmless orb accompanied by a calming tactile warmth, slowly rewiring her fear response.

2. **Nightmare Prevention & Sleep Cycle Optimization:**
   *Story:* Mark suffers from stress-induced nightmares that ruin his sleep architecture. Lucidia maps his brain waves and detects the onset of a night terror. Instantly, the Neural Intervention Mode activates, gently manipulating the auditory sensory inputs within the dream (playing a familiar, soothing song directly into his auditory cortex) to steer the dream away from trauma without waking him up.

3. **Dream Decoding & Emotional Reflection:**
   *Story:* Elena wakes up feeling anxious but doesn't know why. She opens the **DreamBank** in Lucidia. The app has reconstructed the emotional and sensory themes of her night, showing that she experienced high levels of spatial disorientation (vestibular sense) and cold tactile sensations. She reviews the AI-powered insights, realizes it correlates with her recent career change anxiety, and uses the app's *BreathingScreen* to ground herself.

## 📊 How do users manage the new information?

Lucidia features a strictly organized, Apple-inspired interface to prevent information overload:
- **The Dashboard (Home & Stats):** High-level daily sleep scores, sleep architecture graphs, and recovery metrics. Information is surfaced gracefully, prioritizing what matters today.
- **DreamBank & Sensory Analysis:** A dedicated space where dreams are decoded into readable narratives and sensory data (Visual, Tactile, Olfactory, Auditory). Users can review the sensory intensity of their dreams via clean, interactive visualizer components.
- **Neural Intervention Modal:** The core interaction for sensory manipulation. Users are presented with AI-suggested "Phobia Targets" and "Sensory Overrides." Crucially, **users must manually approve** any intervention. They build a personalized "playlist" of sensory manipulations that the BCI will execute during specific REM stages.

---

## 🛡️ What safeguards are in place?

Manipulating the subconscious mind carries profound ethical and psychological risks. Lucidia implements strict safeguards:

1. **Strict Manual Consent (The "Opt-In" Rule):** The BCI will *never* alter a dream or apply a sensory override without explicit, prior manual approval from the user while they are awake and conscious.
2. **On-Device Processing (Privacy):** Neural data is the most intimate data imaginable. All cognitive analysis and decoding happens strictly on-device using advanced post-quantum cryptography. No dream data is ever sent to the cloud.
3. **Emergency Wake Protocol (Fail-safes):** If the BCI detects extreme physiological distress (e.g., heart rate exceeding safe thresholds) that fails to respond to soothing sensory overrides, the hardware initiates a gentle, escalating tactile and auditory wake sequence to pull the user out of the nightmare safely.
4. **Reality Anchoring:** To prevent dissociation, users must complete a quick reality-check calibration exercise on their device upon waking, confirming they are fully conscious and differentiating the dream memories from reality.
